# 04 - Sweet Shop

## Problem

Shopping and budgeting activities can feel form-like for children. A small game can make choosing within a visible budget more playful and concrete.

## Approach

The browser demo gives the player 20 coins and a list of treats with different prices. Players add items while they have enough coins, review the bag, and check out to see the remaining balance.

## Current Scope

- Runs locally in the browser with a fixed catalog and in-memory state.
- Uses play coins only; it has no payment flow or real purchases.
- Demonstrates a product interaction, not a validated learning outcome.

## Evaluation Ideas

Test whether children understand the remaining budget, can recover from a choice, and find the interaction clear and enjoyable. No user testing or measured results are claimed.
