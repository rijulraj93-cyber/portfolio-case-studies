# 05 - Decision Copilot

## Concept

Decision Copilot explores how an AI-assisted workflow could help cross-functional teams frame consequential decisions, compare options, make evidence and trade-offs visible, and preserve a human-approved rationale.

## Intended Workflow

1. Frame the decision, goal, constraints, stakeholders, and criteria.
2. Compare options using traceable evidence while identifying assumptions and unknowns.
3. Let people review, revise, approve, or reject any recommendation.
4. Record the human-approved rationale and revisit outcomes later.

## Principles and Boundaries

- People own the final decision; no consequential action is automatic.
- Evidence, assumptions, and missing information should remain visible.
- The sample is a concept using synthetic/demo material, not a live AI product.
- No production integrations, measured impact, or decision-quality results are claimed.
- This is an independent portfolio concept, not an official Microsoft product or endorsement.

## Validation Ideas

Test whether users can frame a decision, understand the evidence and trade-offs, and identify when information is missing. Candidate measures require definitions and baselines before use; none have been measured.
